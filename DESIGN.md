# Design contract

Read this before writing or changing any UI — human or agent.

These are rules, not suggestions. When a rule and a "nicer looking" idea disagree, the rule wins. Consistency across every screen beats a clever one.

`src/components/ui/**` is vendored shadcn — treat it as a third-party kit and restyle via tokens rather than rewriting it.

**Tokens live in `apps/frontend/src/styles/global.css`.** Components read colour, radius, shadows and fonts from there, never from this file. This file says how to _use_ them. The values come from the Figma collection „HobbyHub tokens“ in [HobbyHub-design](https://www.figma.com/design/dGYGqD8BjC1rYlbfjCpxWD/HobbyHub-design) (page „Design system“), with the same names (`color/hero` → `--hero`, `bg-hero`). When Figma changes, update `global.css` — never patch a colour in a component.

> **New brand (approved by the PO 3.–4. 11. 2026, UX audit #173).** `global.css` and `tokens.test.ts` follow the token table below, the text styles and the capacity hatch; every class in the table exists. The components and screens are rebuilt in parts under #195 — a screen not rebuilt yet may still use a kit name (`bg-muted`, `border-border`…) where the table has its own token; new or changed UI uses the tokens below. Figma holds the approved design: if code and Figma disagree, Figma wins and the code is the bug. Tailwind does not warn about a missing class — the element just stays unstyled — so take names from this table, not from memory. Screens in Figma are illustrative — the data on them need not match the seed.

## Colour

- **Never hardcode a colour.** No `#hex`, no `bg-blue-500`, no `rgb()`. Use the semantic classes only (table below, including the kit aliases at its end).
- **Contrast is not negotiable.** Text at least 4.5:1 (large text 3:1). **Every non-text element at least 3:1 against what is around it**: icons, badges, the capacity track, map pins, rating stars, dividers, and the _edge_ of any surface — a tile, a badge, a card, an outline button, a dialog or a bottom sheet over the overlay. If a fill cannot reach 3:1 on its own, it gets an outline token (`tile-border`, `primary-edge`, `control-border`, `card-border`). The token pairs are tested in `src/styles/tokens.test.ts`; a new pair gets a test. There are no "it looks fine" exceptions.
- **`primary` (lime) is a fill, never a text or icon colour.** Text on lime is `primary-foreground`. On a light surface lime always carries the `primary-edge` outline (lime on cream or white is about 1.1:1). Lime appears only on: the one primary button per view (one exception: the landing page, Figma A-01, A-01d, A-01p, A-01pd, has a second one, „Založit akci“ in the „Nic pro tebe?“ call-out at its bottom — the band's „Prohlédnout akce“ is the page's main action, the call-out is the way out when nothing above fits, far enough apart that the two never compete), the „Chybí“ badge, the „Chybí lidé“ map pin, the ring of a map cluster with an event where people are missing, the capacity hatch, the „Koná se“ stamp, the active item of the desktop header and the bottom nav (pill), the active quick-pick underline on the dark band, the highlighter under a section keyword, the logo's ring. On the dark band of the landing page (Figma A-01, A-01d) also the second line of the title („Třeba ty.“, „na tebe čeká.“), and in „Jak to funguje“ its keyword, the step labels and the dashed line between the steps — lime on `hero` has the contrast of text. Nowhere else — lime is the brand's signal, not decoration.
- **`secondary` (quiet green) always comes with `tile-border`** — the fill alone is 1.2–1.3:1 against the card. Used for: activity tags, the „N volných míst“ and „Jdu“ badges, avatars, activity tiles, the date tile, the „Máš místo“ block, the days inside a picked range in the calendar.
- **`destructive` only for destructive actions and errors.** Never for emphasis. Text on it is `destructive-foreground`.
- Body text is `foreground`; supporting text is `muted-foreground`. There is no third level.
- **The app has a light mode only** (PO 10. 11. 2026). No dark mode, no `.dark` tokens, no theme switcher, no `dark:` classes — never add them back (removed in #297). Never use `text-white`/`bg-black` either; colour comes from the tokens alone. No file in `src` has a `dark:` class or a `.dark` selector, and `index.html` has one `theme-color`, the band's `hero` (`tokens.test.ts` checks both). Text and icons on a filled surface use that surface's `-foreground` token. Form fields (input, textarea, select) sit on `card`, as in Figma. The one exception is the kit's `bg-black/50` overlay behind dialogs and bottom sheets: darkening the page keeps the sheet's edge above 3:1.

## Layout

- **Every top-level page starts with the dark green band** (`PageBand`, Figma „Úvodní pás“), right under the header, which sits on the band too: back link if any, H1, one supporting line, the page's main action on the right (desktop), and tabs on its bottom edge (`BandTabs` — Moje akce, Skupiny, later the quick picks on /akce). Section pages (Akce, Skupiny, Moje akce) put the logo mark before the H1, and from `xl` the big logo motif in `hero-pattern` rises from the bottom edge (the lime ring is the missing one — „parta, které chybí jeden“); narrower, the ring would run under the text. The lime ring never sits under text; the faint `hero-pattern` dots may (the landing page's title runs over them in Figma A-01d — `on-hero` keeps its contrast on `hero-pattern`, tokens.test.ts). **Tabs never run over the motif** (#298): on /akce, whose six quick picks reach under it, the motif rises from the tabs' top line instead of the band's edge (`PageBand isMotifAboveTabs`); elsewhere it stays on the edge, where lifted the ring would reach „Založit akci“. On a phone a section band is the mark, the H1 and the tabs, with the corner motif above the tabs — its lower dot and the ring rising from the tabs' line on the right, as on desktop (Figma A-02, A-07, C-01; not under 360 px, where the dot would reach the title); forms and management shrink it to one bar with the arrow and the title (Figma Header/Sub). The event detail (`PageBand variant="detail"`, Figma A-05, A-05d): on a phone the Header/Sub bar („Detail akce“, „Sdílet“), the activity photo edge to edge, then the band with the activity, the title and the stamp; on desktop the band with the back link, the activity, the title and one line — when, where and how full („do minima chybí 1“, `occupancyInBand`) — and the photo at the top of the content. The group page (Figma A-08, A-08d) is laid out the same, with a photo without a date and „Neveřejná skupina · Praha 2 · 5 členů“ as its line, on a phone too (under the title). Both management pages (Figma D-03d, D-07d) have that band on desktop, the line ending „Pořádáš ty“ / „Spravuješ ty“; on a phone they have no band — the Header/Sub bar is their H1 („Správa akce“, „Správa skupiny“) and the card follows, sign-in and sign-up put the H1 on the page. The back link (`BackLink`) names the page it really returns to („Moje akce“, „Akce“, „Skupina“), „Zpět“ where that page is unknown, and the page's own `label` only on a shared link with no history (N9). Inside the band the `on-hero` utility switches the focus ring and the kit's hover surface to the band's tokens. Content below the band sits on `background`.
- **Cards are for separable items in a list** — an event, a group, a search result. They are not page sections and not layout tools.
- **Never nest a card inside a card.** If content needs grouping inside a card, use spacing and a hairline divider.
- **A page is not a card.** Page content sits on `background` on the 1200 px grid (`PAGE` in `src/lib/pageLayout.ts`), not inside a floating panel.
- **A detail's action panel is the one bordered section** (Figma: Panel akce A-05d, Panel skupiny A-08d). On desktop the facts and the main action sit on the right in a `rounded-2xl border border-card-border bg-card p-6` panel; on a phone there is no panel, the facts follow the title and the main action sits in a bar above the bottom navigation (`sticky bottom-16`, Figma: Lišta s akcí). It holds no cards.
- **Every page keeps the app chrome.** The header is present on every route, including login and sign-up.
- Forbidden by default: the centred-hero-plus-three-feature-cards layout, carousels, and any section whose only purpose is to look full.
- **No big empty areas on desktop** (PO). Use the width for real content: a form gets two columns (D-01d „Co a kdy“ | „Kde“, D-06d), sign-in and sign-up show the nearest events next to the form, the public profile has two columns, event grids have three columns.

## Spacing

Use the scale only: `1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 16` (Tailwind units — 4px…64px; `5` = 20 px is Figma's padding of the grid card and the band). No arbitrary values like `p-[13px]`. Related things get `gap-2`/`gap-3`; separate blocks get `gap-6`/`gap-8`. Half steps (`0.5`, `1.5`, `2.5`, `3.5`) only inside the small brand components where Figma sets them — the date tile, the „min. N“ pill, a tile's corner inset, the gap between two lines of a label — never for layout.

## Type

- One `h1` per page. Headings step down without skipping.
- **Two typefaces.** Bricolage Grotesque (`font-heading`) is the display face: page titles (H1 in the band), section headings, event card titles, the date tile numbers, the „Koná se“ stamp and the wordmark. Everything else — body, labels, buttons, inputs, badges, meta lines — is Geist (`font-sans`).
- Text styles mirror Figma (desktop / mobile): Hero 80 / 40 ExtraBold; Title 48 / 30 ExtraBold; Feature 28 / 24 Bold; event card title 20 Bold (grid) and 17 Bold (row, max 2 lines); date 36 / 26 / 20 ExtraBold. Geist: card heading 17 SemiBold (a heading inside a content column or panel, a bottom sheet's header, a group card's title), label large 16 SemiBold (Figma Label/Button large: a large button, the „Máš místo“ title), lead 20 / 16, intro 17 (desktop: the line under the H1 in the band or under a section heading), body 15, small 14, caption 13, tag 12 SemiBold. They are mapped once, in `global.css`, never per component: the display styles are utilities that carry face, weight and the desktop size from `md` (`type-hero`, `type-title`, `type-feature`, `type-card-title`, `type-row-title`, `type-card-heading`, `type-date-l/m/s`, `type-wordmark`, `type-stamp`, `type-lead`, `type-intro`, `type-step`); the Geist sizes are `text-base` (16, label large only), `text-body` (15), `text-sm` (14), `text-caption` (13) and `text-xs` (12).
- **Section headings have one highlighted keyword** („Tady **chybíš**“, „Tento **víkend**“): `highlight-text` with the `highlight-marker` underneath — the `highlight-keyword` utility on a `<span>` around the word. One keyword per heading, never a whole line.
- Sentence case everywhere. No ALL CAPS except the small step label in „Jak to funguje“ (Figma text style Label/Step).

## Copy

- **The app says „ty“ (tykání), everywhere** — buttons, errors, empty states, toasts, e-mails, API messages the app shows. Never „vy“.
- **Keep it gender-neutral.** The app does not know anyone's gender: no past tense or adjectives in the second person („jsi přihlášený“), and no gendered nouns or verbs about other people („organizátorka“, „Petra zorganizovala“). Write „Máš místo.“, „Organizátor · ★ 4,5“, „Jak hodnotíš organizátora?“.
- **Short, and always with a next step.** An empty state says in one sentence what would be here; a second sentence may have personality and points somewhere („V okolí teď nic není. Buď první – založ akci a dej o ní vědět.“).
- **Numbers, not words**, when the app generates the sentence: „5 z 10 už jde, do minima chybí 1.“
- **Personal naming** for picks made for the user: „Tady chybíš“, „Podle tvých zájmů“, „Tady se na tebe čeká.“ Never address the user by name: the app cannot decline names reliably into the vocative („Eva Malá“ → „Evo“?), and the nominative („Eva, …“) reads oddly in Czech.
- Ratings always show the count: „★ 4,5 (6 hodnocení)“; with no rating yet „zatím bez hodnocení“.
- Content people write themselves (event and group descriptions) stays as they wrote it.

## Surfaces

- **Borders are hairlines**: one `border`, never two adjacent ones. Cards and panels `border-card-border`, inputs and chips `border-input` (the kit alias of `input-border`), outline buttons and neutral badges `border-control-border`, green surfaces `border-tile-border`.
- **Radius:** buttons and inputs `rounded-lg` (8); tiles and photos inside cards `rounded-xl` (12); cards, panels, the map and the „Nic pro tebe?“ call-out `rounded-2xl` (16). Pills (`rounded-full`) are for chips, badges, tags, quick-pick counts and avatars, never for a button. One radius per level: a nested element gets the smaller step (the event card is `rounded-2xl`, its photo and date tile `rounded-xl`), but elements on the same level never mix radii.
- **Shadows are rare.** Only for things that genuinely float — dropdowns and menus, dialogs, toasts, the selected map pin (Figma effect „Elevation/Float“). Cards and inputs do not get shadows. Floating things still need their edge: dialogs carry `card-border`.

## Forms

- Every input has a visible `<label>`. Placeholders are examples, never labels.
- A password field keeps the browser's own „show password“ eye (Edge) clear of the text — `global.css` gives `::-ms-reveal` a gap (#298). The app adds no eye of its own.
- Validation runs client-side _and_ server-side. The client message appears under the field.
- The submit button shows a pending state and is disabled while submitting.
- Never disable a submit button just because the form is untouched — let the user try and show them what's wrong.

## Every list has three states

Loading, empty, and error — all three, always. An empty list renders an empty state with one sentence saying what would appear here, not a blank area. An error renders the message and a way to retry.

Data refreshes itself: refetch when the tab regains focus, and invalidate the query after a mutation. A manual "reload" button in normal UI means one of those is missing — retry belongs in the error state only.

## Accessibility (the floor, not the ceiling)

- Buttons are `<button>`, links are `<a>`/`<Link>`. Never a `div` with `onClick`.
- Every icon-only control has an `aria-label`. Rating stars are a radio group („3 z 5 hvězd“).
- A control's name contains the text it shows (WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name): the avatar button showing „PN“ is „PN – Petra Nováková, uživatelské menu“ (the shown text first), not „Uživatelské menu“.
- Tabs point at a panel that exists: the content they switch is their `TabsContent` (on /akce the list and the map). A panel with a control in it is no Tab stop of its own (WAI-ARIA tabs): Tab goes from the tab to that control, so the kit's `TabsContent` sets `tabIndex={-1}` there for every page. A panel with only text (an empty list) stays a stop, with the `ring` outline. On /akce, with no tab active, the panel takes the page title as its name.
- Focus rings are never removed (`ring` token), and they are drawn in the token's full colour: `ring-ring`, `outline-ring`, `ring-destructive` on an invalid field — never `ring-ring/50` (2.45:1 on the page, under 3:1; #298). On the band the `on-hero` utility switches `ring` to `on-hero`. tokens.test.ts checks every file in `src`. Where the kit marks the focused or picked row only by `accent` (1.29:1 on `card`) — a menu item, a Select option, a Command option — the row also gets an inset `ring-ring`: on `focus-visible` in menus and Select, on the selected option in Command lists.
- Touch targets at least 40 × 40 px (rating stars, map pins).
- Motion only under `motion-safe:` (the „Máš místo“ dot, the capacity fill). Stripes never move — moving stripes read as „loading“.

After the user moves the map — never after the map fits itself to the pins — „Hledat v této oblasti“ (outline, `RotateCw`) floats at the top of the map; it limits the list and the map to the area in view (`?oblast=`), which replaces the locality: the locality chip says „Výřez mapy“ and picking a locality or „Zrušit výběr“ ends the area search.
- **Quick picks** (Figma: Tab/Rychlý výběr) — „Všechny akce“, „Tady chybíš“, „Tento víkend“, „Podle tvých zájmů“ (signed in), „Kousek od tebe“, „Od dobře hodnocených“ as tabs with a count pill, in the band on /akce. On the band the active one has a lime underline; on light surfaces a `foreground` underline. `BandTabsItem surface="page"` is the light one (the landing page's „Pro tebe“, A-01p, A-01pd: Tady chybíš, Tento víkend, Podle tvých zájmů, Kousek od tebe, Od dobře hodnocených — only the picks that have events, the pill counts the cards under it). They differ from filter chips, which only set a value. A strip that does not fit scrolls sideways and reaches the screen's edge, so a tab scrolled past the start is cut by the screen, never mid-page; where a strip starts beside something (the logo mark before „Pro tebe“), the tab fades out in the gap (`fade-start`) instead of being cut beside it (#298). „Zrušit vše“ appears only when a filter was changed by hand beyond the selected pick; with just a pick on it is „Zrušit výběr“, which leads to „Všechny akce“ — the pick lights up its chips („Chybí lidé“), so there is always a way out next to them. „Řadit“ above the list is always a menu: without a locality to measure from „Nejmenší vzdálenost“ is off and the menu says why, never a plain label that looks like the button. On /akce (#195, PR #279) a pick only sets the existing filters, kept in the URL with `?vyber=`: „Tady chybíš“ = Chybí lidé + okolí (+10 km unless one is on) + one's interests, without events one already goes to; „Tento víkend“ = the weekend's dates; „Kousek od tebe“ = okolí, nearest first (only with a known locality); „Podle tvých zájmů“ and „Od dobře hodnocených“ follow the landing page's rules. The locality and its okolí hold for every tab. The count is what the tab shows („200+“ at the API's limit). Changing a filter the pick set leaves the tab and the band says „Akce podle tvých filtrů“.
- **Locality chip** (`LocalityFilter`, #55) — on /akce and /skupiny; the landing page's sections start in the same default locality (#55, `useLocality`). It always names the locality the list shows: the visitor's pick, else the home locality from the profile, else a guess from the IP, else „Celé Česko“ — never a fixed Praha. The default is no filter, so its chip is light (Figma A-02): „Praha · podle polohy“ for a guess, the name for a home locality, „Celé Česko“ when nothing is known; a pick turns it dark. A pick is remembered in the browser only; „Zrušit výběr“ in the panel (a district: „Celá Praha“) and „Zrušit vše“ return to the default, and „Zrušit vše“ appears only once something was picked.
- **Okolí, search and distance** (#57, Figma A-02gd, A-02d, A-02j) — on /akce the locality panel has „Okolí: Jen obec / +10 km / +25 km / +50 km“ between its search field and the list, as a single-choice group of chips (`ToggleGroup`, dark when picked); it applies with the panel's button. Okolí is a filter: the chip says „Praha 8 · +10 km“ on the dark fill, the list holds events at most that far from the locality's centre, ordered nearest first, and each card's line ends with the distance („· 1,3 km“) — whenever the list is ordered by distance, okolí or not, so the order explains itself. Celé Česko and a searched map area have no centre: no okolí, no distance order. „Hledat akci nebo aktivitu“ is a pill-shaped field at the right end of the desktop filter bar; on a phone the header's „Hledat“ opens it above the chips. A list as long as the API's limit says „Víc než 200 akcí“.
- **Panel „Teď chybí jeden“** (Figma: Panel/Teď chybí jeden) — in the landing page band: photo + date tile, a label with the logo („Teď chybí jeden · za 2 dny“), title, place, one sentence with numbers, the capacity bar and an outline „Doplnit partu“. Edge `on-hero-muted`. It never disappears (Figma A-01 · Panel – bez akce): with events but none missing people „Všechny party jsou kompletní“ and „Akce s volnými místy“; with nothing upcoming „Buď první“ and „Zaregistrovat se“ (visitor) or „Založit akci“ (signed in). While loading its skeleton, on an error the message and „Zkusit znovu“ in its place.
- **Activity tile and tag** (Figma: Activity tile, Tag/Activity) — the tile is the category icon (`secondary` + `tile-border`; `tile-on-hero` on the band): event detail header, the activity picker, cards without a photo. The tag shows the activity name (second level), not the category.
- **Rating stars** (Figma: Hodnocení/Hvězdičky, Hvězda) — input for 1–5 stars, 40 px targets. Full star `foreground`, empty star outlined in `control-border` — never lime (lime fails 3:1). Showing an average stays text: „★ 4,5 (6 hodnocení)“.
- **Navigation** — desktop header (Figma: Header/App desktop): logo, Akce, Skupiny, Moje akce (signed in), on the right the avatar, or „Přihlásit se“ + „Registrovat“. The active item is a lime pill. The mobile header (Figma: Header/Screen) has the logo and the screen's action. Mobile bottom navigation (Figma: Nav/Bottom): icon + label, the active item has a lime pill behind the icon; inactive icon and label `muted-foreground`. The map is part of Akce, never its own item.
- **Založit akci** — lives in Moje akce only (desktop right of the title, mobile floating above the bottom navigation, never covering the last card). Never in the header, never a page-level button on /akce; the one exception is /akce finding nothing (`EmptyEvents`), where it is the secondary next step. **Založit akci skupiny** is a different action: on the group page, for the group's admins only, an outline button under „Spravovat skupinu“ (Figma D-01g, #70).
- **Menus** (sort on /akce, Figma A-02j) — the kit's `bg-popover` (= `card`) with `border-card-border rounded-2xl` with „Elevation/Float“; the selected option has a check. Sorting: „Nejbližší termín“ (default) and „Nejmenší vzdálenost“ (from the centre of the chosen locality; not offered for the whole country).

## When you are unsure

Prefer less: fewer borders, fewer boxes, fewer font sizes, more whitespace. If a screen feels plain, the fix is usually better spacing and clearer hierarchy, not another container — the brand lives in the band, the photos, the capacity bar and the voice, not in extra decoration.
